What you'll get

What the Health Check Does

  • Helps you identify which of the 12 practices your church or group is already strong in.
  • Shows you the weak spots — the practices that are missing or inconsistent.
  • Gives you clear next steps instead of a vague health score.
  • Helps leaders know exactly what to practice next, together.
How it works

Three Minutes, Three Steps

1

Answer 12 questions

One for each practice from Acts 2:36-47 — belief, repentance, baptism, prayer, giving, and the rest.

2

See your results

Your answers map straight onto the Church Waffle grid, so you can see strengths and gaps at a glance.

3

Get next steps

Walk away with 1-2 practices to focus on with your group this month.
