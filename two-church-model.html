The Two Church Model

Why church health and church structure are two different questions.

Short Summary

The Two Church Model separates two ideas that usually get lumped together: what a church practices, and how a church is structured. A group can be structurally simple — a handful of people in a living room — and still be fully practicing the commands of Jesus. A group can also have buildings, staff, and programs and be missing basic practices entirely.

Why It Matters

If you only think about structure — how big, how organized, how official — you can miss whether a group is actually healthy. And if you only think about health without a plan for structure, healthy practices stay stuck in one room instead of multiplying into new groups. You need language for both.

The connection

Two Questions, One Picture

The Church Waffle helps you see what your church is practicing. The Two Church Model helps you think about the structure that allows those practices to multiply. Use them together: diagnose health with the Waffle, then think about structure with the Two Church Model.
