What it is

What is the Church Waffle?

The Church Waffle is a simple grid of 12 practices found in the New Testament church. Instead of guessing whether your group is "healthy," you look at each practice one at a time and ask a straight question: are we actually doing this, or just talking about it?

It works for house churches, small groups, and traditional congregations. You don't need special training to use it — just a willingness to look honestly at what's happening in your group.

The problem

Attendance isn't the same as health

Most groups that call themselves a church can tell you how many people showed up last Sunday. Few can tell you whether they're actually practicing what Jesus commanded.

Good teaching and a strong turnout don't tell you if a group is baptizing new believers, praying together, giving generously, or making disciples who make more disciples.

The Church Waffle gives you a simple, visual way to ask the real question: are we practicing the commands of Jesus together — not just meeting together?
